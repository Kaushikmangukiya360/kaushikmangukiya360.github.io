## Overview

Vicurs unifies inventory, storefront, and customer support into a single AI-assisted commerce platform, aimed at small and mid-sized merchants who currently juggle disconnected tools.

## The challenge

Merchants were losing sales because inventory, storefront, and support lived in three separate systems that didn't talk to each other — stock would sell out on one channel while still listed as available on another, and support agents had no visibility into order history.

## The approach

- Built a unified data layer in Python/FastAPI syncing inventory across channels in near real-time.
- Added a GenAI support layer that answers common order questions directly from live order data, escalating only when needed.
- Designed the storefront in React with server-rendered product pages for SEO.

## The result

Early merchants on the platform saw fewer overselling incidents and cut support response time significantly by resolving common questions automatically before they reached a human agent.

## Stack

Python, FastAPI, React, OpenAI, MongoDB.
