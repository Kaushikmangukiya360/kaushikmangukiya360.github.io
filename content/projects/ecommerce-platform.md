## Overview

A full-stack storefront built for a client moving off a rigid, hard-to-customize e-commerce SaaS onto a platform they could extend themselves.

## The challenge

The client's previous platform made it difficult to customize checkout flow or add merchandising logic like personalized recommendations without expensive third-party plugins.

## The approach

- Built the storefront and admin panel on Node.js with MongoDB for flexible product/catalog schemas.
- Added a recommendation module using purchase-history similarity to surface related products on product pages and cart.
- Migrated existing catalog and order history with zero downtime during cutover.

## The result

The client gained full control over checkout and merchandising logic, and the recommendation module measurably lifted average order value on product pages where it was shown.

## Stack

Node.js, Express, MongoDB, React.
